## In this session

We host Assemblymember Gail Pellerin, who represents California Assembly District 28 and chairs the Assembly Elections Committee, for a conversation about her work on AI legislation. She will begin with a short presentation, followed by a conversation with Simone and student Q&A. We then compare evidence on persuasion and political action, and evaluate one election-AI rule through its mechanism, enforcement and democratic trade-offs.

## Questions we'll tackle

- How might AI change who has an advantage in a campaign?
- What problem should an election-AI rule target, and how would we know it works?
- What separates evidence of persuasion from evidence of political behavior or election results?
- Who must comply with a rule, who enforces it, and what trade-offs follow?
