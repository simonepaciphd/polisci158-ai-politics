## In this session

We examine how control of AI inputs can become political power. We distinguish six channels of firm influence, then compare model access and governance coalitions. Finally, we ask how a model should respond when a lab, a user and a government make competing demands.

## Questions we'll tackle

- Why can concentrated resources help firms organize politically?
- Which channel connects a firm's resources to a policy or electoral decision?
- What evidence distinguishes participation from successful influence?
- What is the difference between open weights, open source AI and hosted access?
- Whose interests should a model serve when demands conflict?

### Review questions

- Identify an actor, its interest, an influence channel and one observation in the NY-12 case. What additional evidence would test influence?
